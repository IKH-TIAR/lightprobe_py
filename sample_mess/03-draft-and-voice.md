# Draft Scene and Voice Sample

## Draft scene

The rain began before Mara reached the harbour, soft at first, then hard
enough to turn every roof into a drum. She kept one hand over the compass in
her pocket. It was warm. That was the wrong thing about it. Brass should have
been cold in weather like this.

At the end of the pier, Tomas waited beneath the broken customs sign. He did
not wave. He only looked past her, toward the lighthouse, where the dark
windows watched the sea.

"You came alone," he said.

"You told me to."

"I told you not to bring Edda."

Mara took out the compass. Its needle trembled once and pointed up the cliff.
For a moment, beneath the wind and the rain, she heard her brother say her
name.

## Voice notes

The author wants tense, atmospheric scenes with concrete sensory details.
Dialogue should be restrained and carry hidden disagreement. Avoid jokes
during supernatural discoveries. Keep the prose clear rather than ornate.
