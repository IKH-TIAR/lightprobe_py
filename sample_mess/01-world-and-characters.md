# The Lantern Coast

## World rules

The city of Bellwether is built on cliffs above a black sea. Every night at
midnight, the lighthouse beam turns green for exactly one minute. During that
minute, people who are standing near the water can hear one sentence spoken by
someone who is far away.

The green light cannot reveal the future. It can only carry words that someone
has already spoken.

The old lighthouse has been closed for twelve years. The official reason is
that the stairs are unsafe, but the harbour master knows that the lamp room
still turns on by itself.

## Characters

Mara Venn is seventeen and repairs watches in her aunt's shop. She is practical,
impatient, and secretly afraid of deep water. Her older brother Iven vanished
at sea three years ago.

Tomas Reed is eighteen and works as a messenger between the cliff villages. He
collects broken keys and believes every lock was made for a reason. Tomas says
he does not remember Iven, but Mara thinks he is lying.

Edda Venn is Mara's aunt and the owner of the watch shop. She knows that Iven
was investigating the lighthouse before he disappeared. She has hidden his
notebook inside the shop's oldest clock.
