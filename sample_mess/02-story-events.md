# Story Notes: The First Green Minute

## Main events

1. Mara hears Iven's voice during the green lighthouse minute. He says:
   "Do not let Tomas open the door."

2. The next morning, Mara finds Iven's brass compass inside the oldest clock.
   The compass points toward the lighthouse instead of north.

3. Mara and Tomas climb to the closed lighthouse during a storm. Tomas admits
   that Iven asked him to guard the lighthouse door before he disappeared.

4. The lighthouse door opens only when Mara places the brass compass into its
   keyhole.

5. Inside the lamp room, they find a wall covered with names and dates. The
   newest date is tomorrow.

## Loose questions

- Why did Iven trust Tomas with the lighthouse?
- Who is writing the names on the wall?
- What happens if the green light carries a sentence from someone who is dead?

## Continuity note
